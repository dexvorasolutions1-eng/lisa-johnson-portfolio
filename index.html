About Lisa

Hi, I'm Lisa Johnson — an anime style illustrator with a love for soft lighting, expressive characters and dreamy color palettes. I've spent years bringing commissions to life for clients who want their characters, OCs and favorite fandoms drawn with care.

Whether you need a chibi sticker, a full body illustration or a custom emote set, I work closely with you from sketch to final render to make sure every piece feels like you.

  • Style: Anime / semi-realistic
  • Turnaround: 3–10 business days
